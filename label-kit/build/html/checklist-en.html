Label compliance checklist (EU)

Cosmetics (Reg. EC 1223/2009) · Food supplements (Reg. EU 1169/2011, Dir. 2002/46/EC) · Candles (EN 15494, CLP) · Packaging (national environmental labelling, e.g. Italy D.Lgs. 116/2020, France Triman)

Practical guidance for designers, not legal advice. Have the responsible person (for cosmetics, whoever notified the product on CPNP) verify texts and claims before printing.

A. Cosmetics: what must appear (Art. 19, Reg. 1223/2009)

  • Name and address of the responsible person in the EU.
  • Country of origin if made outside the EU.
  • Nominal content by weight or volume (e.g. 50 ml). The ℮ mark is optional (metrological control). Exempt: under 5 g/5 ml, free samples, single-use.
  • Date of minimum durability with hourglass symbol if ≤ 30 months ("Best used before end of" + month/year), or PAO (open-jar + months) if > 30 months.
  • Precautions for use and particular warnings (Annexes III–VI). If they don't fit: hand-on-book symbol + leaflet.
  • Batch number.
  • Function of the product, unless obvious.
  • Ingredients list headed "Ingredients", INCI names, descending order by weight (under 1 % any order). Colourants by CI number. Fragrance allergens listed individually above threshold (0.001 % leave-on / 0.01 % rinse-off). Nanomaterials marked "[nano]".
  • Language of the member state where sold for content, durability, precautions and function. INCI stays INCI.
  • Text legible, visible, indelible. No legal minimum height for cosmetics: use ≥ 1.2 mm x-height anyway.

Claims you cannot make

  • "Heals", "cures", "anti-inflammatory", medicinal effects: that makes it a medicine.
  • "Free from parabens" and similar denigrating "free from" claims (Reg. 655/2013 common criteria).
  • "Dermatologically tested", "hypoallergenic", "98 % natural": only with documented evidence.

B. Food supplements

  • The name "food supplement" (in the local language, exact wording).
  • Names of the categories of substances or an indication of their nature.
  • Recommended daily portion.
  • Warning: "Do not exceed the recommended daily dose".
  • Statement that supplements should not be used as a substitute for a varied diet.
  • Warning: "Keep out of reach of young children".
  • Ingredients list, allergens emphasised, nutrition table per daily portion with % NRV where applicable.
  • Net quantity, best-before date, lot, storage conditions, name and address of the food business operator.
  • Minimum x-height 1.2 mm (0.9 mm if largest surface < 80 cm²).
  • Health claims only from the EU register (Reg. 1924/2006). No "cures", "prevents", "slimming".
  • Notification to the national authority before marketing (varies by country).

C. Candles

  • EN 15494 safety pictograms (at least the 3 included in simboli/): never leave unattended, keep away from children and pets, keep away from flammable things. Also in text.
  • Recommended use instructions: trim wick to 5 mm, max 4 hours, do not move while lit, burn on a heat-resistant surface.
  • CLP (Reg. 1272/2008): if the wax + fragrance mixture is classified hazardous (common with essential oils and fragrance loads), you need pictogram, signal word, H/P statements, supplier name and phone, allergens. Ask your fragrance supplier for the SDS.
  • Net weight (g), indicative burn time, lot, producer name and address.

D. Packaging environmental labelling

  • Italy (D.Lgs. 116/2020): for each separable component, the material code (Dec. 97/129/EC), e.g. "Jar: GL 70 · Cap: PP 5 · Label: PAP 22", plus a sorting statement. France: Triman logo + Info-tri. Check the country you sell in.
  • Can sit on the label, outer pack or, for small packs, online via QR/link if indicated.

E. Before sending to print (any product)

  • All mandatory texts present, in the right language
  • No typos in INCI and warnings (copy-paste from the formula, never retype)
  • EAN barcode with quiet zone, black on white, not scaled below 80 %
  • Lot and expiry: space left for inkjet, or included on the label
  • Paper proof applied to the real container: curvature, legibility, overlap
  • Written approval (email) from the client / responsible person before the run
